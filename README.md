# 🍟 McDonald's Mod

### Build burgers, grow ingredients and create your own fast-food restaurant in Minecraft!

McDonald's Mod expands Minecraft with a complete fast-food production system. Grow fresh vegetables, prepare ingredients with reusable kitchen tools, cook burgers, fry chicken and mine Salt Ore hidden underground.

Start with basic ingredients and work your way up to iconic burgers such as the Big Mac, McCrispy and the complete Quarter Pounder family!

---

## ✨ What’s New in Version 1.4.1?

Version 1.4.1 introduces five new burgers, mayonnaise and a complete bacon preparation process.

### New Burgers

- 🍔 **Quarter Pounder with Cheese**
- 🍔 **Double Quarter Pounder with Cheese**
- 🍔 **Quarter Pounder with Cheese Deluxe**
- 🍔 **Daily Double**
- 🥓 **Bacon Quarter Pounder with Cheese**

All burgers have individually balanced hunger and saturation values based on their size and ingredients.

### Mayonnaise

Mayonnaise is a reusable condiment with **8 uses**, just like Ketchup and Mustard.

Craft it using:

- Glass Bottle
- Egg
- 2× Wheat Seeds
- Salt

### Bacon Preparation

Bacon is produced through several preparation stages:

1. **Raw Porkchop + Knife → 3× Raw Bacon**
2. **Raw Bacon + Salt → Salted Raw Bacon**
3. Smelt **Salted Raw Bacon** in a regular Minecraft Furnace to obtain **Bacon**

## 🌱 Crops & Farming

Grow five different crops to produce your own burger ingredients:

- 🍅 **Tomato Plant** — produces fresh Tomatoes
- 🥒 **Cucumber Plant** — produces Cucumbers used as pickles
- 🧅 **Onion Plant** — produces Onions
- 🌿 **Mustard Plant** — produces Mustard Seeds
- 🥬 **Lettuce Plant** — produces Lettuce and additional Lettuce Seeds

Seeds and plantable ingredients can be discovered by breaking:

- Grass
- Tall Grass
- Ferns
- Large Ferns

There is a **10% chance** of receiving one of the mod’s seeds or plantable ingredients.

## 🛠️ Kitchen Tools

### 🍳 Spatula

The Spatula is used in food preparation and burger recipes. It remains in the crafting grid after use, so it does not need to be crafted again for every meal.

### 🔪 Knife

The Knife is a reusable food-preparation tool with **70 uses**.

It can be used to:

- Cut a Chicken into Chopped Chicken
- Prepare Raw Chicken Fillets
- Shred Lettuce
- Cut a Raw Porkchop into Raw Bacon
- Prepare other ingredients and recipes

The Knife loses one durability point whenever it is used in crafting.

---

## 🍳 Frying Station

The **Frying Station** is the heart of your fast-food kitchen. It works similarly to a Furnace but supports special frying recipes.

Use it to prepare:

- 🍟 Fries
- 🍗 Crispy Chicken Fillets
- 🍗 McNuggets

### Features

- Uses regular Furnace fuel
- Lights up while active
- Supports hopper automation
- Uses dedicated frying recipes
- Includes a familiar Furnace-style interface

> Bacon is cooked separately in a regular Minecraft Furnace after being salted.

---

## ⛏️ Salt Ore

Salt Ore generates naturally underground in the Overworld.

Mine it to obtain Salt, which is required for:

- Mayonnaise
- Salted Raw Bacon
- Fries
- Other food preparation recipes

A Stone Pickaxe or better is recommended.
